Chapter one

About me

I'm a Master in Finance student at Chicago Booth. I like hard questions that live in messy data, whether they come from markets, credit risk, or consumers — and whatever the data, I test a result on data it hasn't seen before I trust it. A careful "this doesn't work" is worth more to me than an exciting number I can't defend.

Outside of work, I play competitive badminton doubles and won the 2026 PBC Spring Open women's doubles title. I was a K-pop fan before I was a founder: I turned that into a cross-border merchandise business that reached 4,000+ customers and a 1,500+ member community. I also take photos, practice calligraphy, cook, travel, and speak Mandarin, English, and Cantonese.

Along the way I've worked with credit records at China Post Consumer Finance, ChiNext index returns at Golden Eagle, a 70-year banknote dataset with Professor Barry Eichengreen, Chinese consumer markets at Daxue Consulting, and earnings calls read by LLMs in the Booth × Optiver competition.
